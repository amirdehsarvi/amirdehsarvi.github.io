This study evaluates whether a single dynamic [<sup>18</sup>F]PI-2620 tau-PET recording can predict β-amyloid status while simultaneously staging tau pathology and neurodegeneration. In 129 patients with tauopathies (47 amyloid-positive, 82 amyloid-negative) and 17 healthy controls, kinetic modelling of 60-minute dynamic scans yielded three complementary readouts: tracer clearance, regional tau burden and a perfusion-based marker of neurodegeneration.

**Key Findings:**

* **Amyloid prediction from a tau scan:** cortical tracer clearance was reduced in 3/4-repeat (Alzheimer's-type) compared with 4-repeat tauopathies, discriminating β-amyloid status with an AUC of 0.99 in the primary cohort and 0.98 in validation.
* **One-stop A/T/N staging:** amyloid, tau and neurodegeneration information is obtained from a single imaging session.
* **Clinical relevance:** the approach may reduce radiation exposure, streamline diagnostic workflows and support personalised disease profiling.
